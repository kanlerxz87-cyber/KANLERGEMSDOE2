## Why the result stopped

A registered, SHA-pinned lattice leaves 6,649 uncovered footprint pixels at radius 3 px. For any frozen 37,654-dot placement, at least **31,005/37,654 = 82.341849%** overlap it, above the 70% maximum. With known-fault pixels excluded, 6,514 eligible uncovered cells imply at least **31,140/37,654 = 82.700377%**. N ≤ 21,713 is necessary in the latter domain, not permission to change the budget or evidence of clearance. No exception is stated for the lattice. The pre-placement surface and complete registry were not rechecked; no new surface or dot set was made.

GEMSDOE32's reported 0.2778 is the largest in the table. Its mask equals GEMSDOE25's 44,090 dots after removing 6,436 within 2 px of the mirrored known-fault mask; GEMSDOE36 includes its mask plus 1,200 dots. Thinning might help or hurt depending on hidden labels, so the score's cause is unknown. [Organizer staff](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4) say only exact known-fault pixels are masked; nearby predictions are scored normally and may match new faults. A higher public leaderboard score exists, but it is not evidence this lane can exceed 0.2778. See the [corrected forensic](research/2026-10-27_gmv_forensic_02778.md).

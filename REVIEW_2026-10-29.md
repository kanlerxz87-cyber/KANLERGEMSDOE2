## Hard blockers verified from checkout

1. `registry/preregistration_htcads.json` freezes the output budget at 37,654 dots and a maximum 70% within-3-pixel overlap against each registered detector raster.
2. `evidence/preflight_replay_20261028.json` records a mathematical lower bound of 31,005/37,654 = 82.3418% overlap with one registered spacing-5 witness. This exceeds the literal cap before any model surface or candidate placement; the prior replay is the source, not a fresh calculation in this session.
3. The cloned repository has **no `data/` directory**, and this Python environment has none of numpy, scipy, rasterio, sklearn, lightgbm, pandas, shapely, pyproj, scikit-image or pytest. Therefore no holdout evaluator, raster validator, generator, or test suite could be executed in this session. No cached feature array was present in the checkout.
4. Existing score rows are owner/user-supplied or historical evidence; no current submission-page receipt was found binding GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 to its exact file hash. The official competition training data page requires login per prior documented audit; owner mirrors are not organizer provenance.

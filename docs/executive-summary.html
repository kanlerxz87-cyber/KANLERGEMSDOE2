Why 0.2778 is only reported

Geometry explains a possibility—not causation.

What is reproducibly geometric

The GEMSDOE32 37,654-dot mask equals GEMSDOE25 after removing its 6,436 dots within 2 pixels of the restored known-fault catalogue (XOR = 0). GEMSDOE36 adds 1,200 dots to the GEMSDOE32 mask. GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical in-footprint positive masks.

The nine-site audit found every row’s directional overlap with at least one other site above 70%; maximum nearest-peer overlap is 100.00%.

Why score causation cannot be established

The score 0.2778 is labeled USER/OWNER-REPORTED: the reviewed records do not contain an organizer submission-page receipt tying that score to the pinned TIFF. The official private truth and exact scored file are not available here.

The recorded metric is distance-weighted Tversky (α=0.2, β=0.8) with a 300 m triangular kernel; the known-fault mask is pixel-exact, not a 2-pixel buffer. Pruning catalog-adjacent dots could plausibly remove predictions outside useful hidden-truth support and reduce false-positive cost, but it could also discard nearby distance-weighted credit or increase false negatives, which carry the larger coefficient. Geometry alone cannot identify which pixels affected hidden truth or the score.

Therefore no hidden score, causal gain, “zero true positives” result, or score projection is asserted.

## Scientific grounding and limits

- The [official task](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) is prediction of faults indicative of geothermal resources, **not direct proof of a vent**. Its known-fault catalogue is incomplete. Independent field confirmation is outside this automated submission workflow.
- The [USGS GeoDAWN data release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) describes airborne radiometric and magnetic data, coordinated 3DEP, differing survey specifications and processing; survey geometry and altitude are potential confounds. It marks that release CC0. This does not authenticate the derived peer-cache bytes.
- The official [INGENIOUS GDR compilation](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) is publicly accessible under CC BY 4.0 with attribution; it includes spring/well chemistry, Quaternary faults, and other layers. Do not confuse volcanic vents with geothermal upflow or use catalogue-derived distance columns in held-out folds.
- A [USGS-indexed study of Southern Gabbs Valley](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70201803) reports a blind geothermal system investigated using structural, gravity, magnetic, magnetotelluric and temperature evidence. This supports multimodal hypotheses, not a guarantee that the same proxies generalize throughout the competition area.
- [USGS-indexed structural modeling](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/structural-discontinuities-and-their-control-hydrothermal-systems-great-basin-usa) discusses fault stepovers and terminations as controls on hydrothermal circulation. It does not validate a pixel classifier or establish a leaderboard score.

## Execution constraints before any new candidate

Preregister one exclusive method and fixed budget, restore and hash-check the shared template cache and full parallel registry, and freeze a genuinely fold-local current-best baseline. Screen each input alone on buffered, whole-component held-out folds. Construct all catalogue-dependent features only from each fold's visible faults; mask visible pixels exactly. Check the continuous surface before placement, then compare **every** final dot to **every** registered raster at Euclidean radius 3 px. Stop on the first duplicate; do not omit calibration rasters. Score pooled distance-weighted Tversky (alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, triangular 300 m) with a paired spatial-block 95% CI and withheld-positive count. A holdout is a proxy, never a public leaderboard estimate. At most three experiments or two hours; no automatic slot selection.

The current lattice audit provides a pre-experiment obstruction: its 3-pixel neighborhood covers 5,160,724 of 5,167,373 footprint cells. For any K positive pixels, at most 6,649 can lie outside that neighborhood, so K ≥ 22,164 necessarily exceeds the strict 70% overlap limit. This does **not** prove every smaller candidate fails, or that a lattice match demonstrates scientific plagiarism. It does show why a common 37,654-dot budget cannot pass the literal rule. Repositioning points just to avoid the lattice would invalidate the intended scientific meaning of novelty.

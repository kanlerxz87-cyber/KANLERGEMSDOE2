## Data feasibility, provenance and limits

- The [organizer problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) identifies the fault target, supplied geophysical features and conditional use of licensed external data. The [organizer reference implementation](https://github.com/drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution) explicitly provides a CPU option. A GPU is not an absolute requirement.
- [USGS GeoDAWN](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) identifies the data release, survey blocks, flight specifications, processing and **CC0** rights. The linked GeoTIFF and flight-line products are described on the official page. The locally restored uint8 files are peer-derived, not verified as independently reconstructed from those raw data.
- The [USGS 3DEP page](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program) states that 3DEP products are free and without use restrictions and provides access tools. Actual required tiles and their complete study-area coverage have **not** been retrieved or verified in this sandbox. The organizer's DEM link list is behind [login](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/data/).
- The [DOE Geothermal Data Repository INGENIOUS compilation](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) describes publicly accessible fault, thermal, well, spring, seismic and other layers under CC BY 4.0; use requires attribution and checking each linked resource. Do not include catalogue-derived columns in fold-independent features. Location and temperature sampling are biased toward where people looked.
- Core GeoTIFF mirrors and three derived caches now match pinned peer SHA-256 and the mirrored sample grid; see [data_readiness.json](../evidence/data_readiness.json). A hash verifies equality to that mirror, **not** its official origin, processing quality, licensing or generalization.

## Experiment protocol once the stop rule is resolved

Preassign one exclusive lane and freeze the **complete** registry, cached feature lineage, current best and folds. Test each actual feature alone on withheld positives; AUC above 0.90 is a leakage alarm and a lower AUC does not prove independence. Hide whole connected fault components and a buffer; derive catalogue features from visible faults only; mask visible fault pixels exactly. Use the shared `SegmentHoldout` for pooled distance-weighted Tversky components, a spatial-bootstrap 95% CI and a paired comparison with an independently reconstructed best on identical folds. Check the surface before placement, then final dots, against all registry entries without exemptions. Stop at the first duplicate; never jitter an old file to evade the rule. Use at most three experiments or two hours, and do not spend a competition slot without the separate selector and authenticated allowance check.

Why the answer is “no”

The gate fails before modeling.

A mathematically forced collision is not fixed by hand-picking dots, changing filenames, or calling the lattice a calibration control.

01

One witness blocks every placement at this budget

Within 3 px, the registered witness covers 5,160,724 of 5,167,373 footprint pixels. Only 6,649 pixels lie outside its neighborhood, so at least 31,005 of any 37,654 distinct dots overlap.

This is a precise lower bound for this footprint, witness, radius and frozen dot budget—not a claim that every possible smaller raster fails.

02

No live validation followed

The complete current registry, pre-placement surface, shared cached stack, leakage canary and buffered hide-and-recover evaluator were not replayed. The sandbox has no restored data/ or .cache/ stack.

Not running holdout or fitting is the correct fail-closed result while the hard stop holds.

Why the dotted champion may work

The pinned GEMSDOE32 documentation describes thinning a ridge mask and removing dots within two pixels of known faults. Sparse placement can retain distance-tolerant recall while reducing false-positive mass. That is a plausible metric mechanism, not proof about hidden truth. Distinct file bytes alone do not demonstrate a distinct geological strategy.

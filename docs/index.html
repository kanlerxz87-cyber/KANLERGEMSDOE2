THE CHAMPION · EXPLAINED

Why did H33 B2 lead the supplied list?

Its documented lineage sparsifies a ridge prediction and removes a near-catalogue flank. Under the organizer's 300 m distance-weighted metric, nearby dots may cover the same fault pixel while still adding false-positive mass. Spacing can reduce this redundancy; pruning may help or hurt depending on hidden faults.

This is a plausible mechanism, not causal proof. The hidden labels and a receipt linking the exact file to the reported result are unavailable. Neither a high public score nor a lineament confirms a geothermal vent.

Why GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 may have been higher

The source records describe a nested H19-5 → D2.8 → B1 → B2 ridge family with catalogue-near dots pruned. Under the official distance-weighted Tversky metric (α=0.2, β=0.8; 300 m triangular kernel), thinning can remove low-marginal-credit predictions while retaining nearby kernel coverage, plausibly reducing false-positive mass without losing as much true-positive credit. The source-reported score ladder is consistent with that explanation, but does not prove causality: the score is OWNER-REPORTED, the exact submitted raster is not linked to an organizer receipt, and the hidden truth is not available. The historical alias `h33-h33-2-b2` does not establish that the later H33-A..E module generated that file. See the full metric/lineage review and archived H33 experiment record.

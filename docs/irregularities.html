What still stands in the way.

These are explicit blockers, not requests for a human geological inspector. Automated checks must fail closed.

01 · blocking

Literal novelty contradiction

The registered spacing-5 raster is a calibration control, but the prompt says any registry raster. Its neighborhood forces ≥82.34% overlap at the frozen budget, above the 70% stop. No implicit control exemption has been applied.

02 · blocking

Replay and registry completeness

The prior pre-placement surface and OOF caches are unavailable. A single witness proves failure but cannot prove all-registry uniqueness; the complete current registry and independent holdout/canary replay are not established.

03 · blocking

Score provenance

Historical scores are user/owner reports, not receipts. The legacy site incorrectly called them organizer-confirmed and claimed a mathematical proof of hidden true positives. Both claims are withdrawn.

04 · blocking

Portal and quota

Neither all-finite nor NaN-exterior variants have an acceptance receipt here. The cause of the range error and current weekly quota are unknown; the selector does not provide a positive authorization path.

05 · provenance

Data is restored, not authenticated

Core, external and calibration owner mirrors were restored and hash-pinned. Hashes establish identity to a mirror, not origin at DrivenData, USGS or DOE. The mirror sample also contains known-fault positives despite the page’s “total fault absence” description.

Resolve the literal control conflict without silently weakening it; obtain the authenticated organizer template and a complete frozen registry; replay canaries and spatial holdout; implement quota verification; only then allow a separate selector to approve a file.

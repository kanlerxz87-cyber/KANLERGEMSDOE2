  • IR-KG2S3-DSLUP-RETRACT-04 (high): Independent byte-level re-validation of the Session-2 'OK TO SUBMIT' DSLUP file found 72.69% of its dots within 3 px of gems22-h23-a-dti-optimal-emission-6pct, above the 70% limit.   • IR-KG2S3-LATTICE-06 (medium): The null-calibrated excess (obs - halo)/(1 - halo) against the spacing-5 lattice is sensitive to footprint-boundary avoidance.   • IR-KG2S3-ACCOUNTS-07 (flag-for-user): The official rules allow 'multiple submissions, subject to the limits specified on the competition website (three submissions per week)' (https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf §3.2). The pinned live ledger holds 123 scored rasters across ~70 'sites' within about five weeks. If these entries come from several DrivenData accounts controlled by one person or team, that pattern would exceed the per-competitor limit. Not verifiable from this sandbox. The user should confirm that every account is a distinct eligible competitor or registered team member before using any slot.
  • IR-KG2S3-TARGETS-08 (info): The brief's targets 0.4413 and 0.3195 are stale. The public leaderboard fetched 2026-10-27 shows #1 at 0.4544, #5 at 0.3566, #10 at 0.3379 and #20 at 0.3273. The documents quote the dated leaderboard.

Next steps

  1. Decide which instrument governs promotion. One option is to submit ONE adjudication file, which the protocol currently blocks because no file passes uniqueness. The other is to amend the protocol so that a leave-site-out-validated live-ledger projection, with its error band, can serve as a gate.
  2. Ask the organizers, or watch forums 11563/11584, whether NBMG CC BY 4.0 layers (Quaternary faults, 1:500k geology on the ArcGIS Hub) and USGS-published quadrangle fault plates may be used. Detailed published fault maps are the only identified source of information absent from every registry raster.
  3. If allowed, build a fault-map-distance factor and refit the LLI. H1 says it must improve the leave-site-out MAE by at least 0.001 before any emission.
  4. Train a 1 m LiDAR scarp detector on catalogue scarps (USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM, public domain; not reachable from this sandbox). The 100 m aggregated scarp features carry less than 0.001 of extra live-ledger signal.
  5. Re-audit the H19-5 provenance (GEMSDOE19) for catalogue-label use before it serves as any holdout feature.

Limitations

  • Every live score is USER-REPORTED, with no organizer receipt.
  • LLI projections carry about ±0.025 leave-site-out MAE. On the nine focus files they are biased high by +0.012 on average, and an optimiser-curse bias applies to any candidate emitted against the same model.
  • The registry check covers 360 registry rasters and 124 ledger rasters, not every raster in existence.
  • HIDE-RECOVER and SGMC-CAL measure catalogue-like faults, not the expert-mapped scored population.
01 · blocking

Literal novelty contradiction

The registered spacing-5 raster is a calibration control, but the prompt says any registry raster. Its neighborhood forces ≥82.34% overlap at the frozen budget, above the 70% stop. No implicit control exemption has been applied.

02 · blocking

Replay and registry completeness

The prior pre-placement surface and OOF caches are unavailable. A single witness proves failure but cannot prove all-registry uniqueness; the complete current registry and independent holdout/canary replay are not established.

03 · blocking

Score provenance

Historical scores are user/owner reports, not receipts. The legacy site incorrectly called them organizer-confirmed and claimed a mathematical proof of hidden true positives. Both claims are withdrawn.

04 · blocking

Portal and quota

Neither all-finite nor NaN-exterior variants have an acceptance receipt here. The cause of the range error and current weekly quota are unknown; the selector does not provide a positive authorization path.

05 · provenance

Data is restored, not authenticated

Core, external and calibration owner mirrors were restored and hash-pinned. Hashes establish identity to a mirror, not origin at DrivenData, USGS or DOE. The mirror sample also contains known-fault positives despite the page’s “total fault absence” description.

06 · next session

Reliable promotion pathway

Resolve the literal control conflict without silently weakening it; obtain the authenticated organizer template and a complete frozen registry; replay canaries and spatial holdout; implement quota verification; only then allow a separate selector to approve a file.

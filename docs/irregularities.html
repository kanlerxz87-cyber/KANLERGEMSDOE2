What still stands in the way.

01 · blocking

Literal novelty contradiction

The registered lattice forces at least 82.34% overlap at the assigned budget, above the 70% stop. It is a calibration control, but the protocol says any registry raster. No exemption is authorized.

02 · blocking

Independent replay

A single witness proves failure, not complete novelty. The current full registry, pre-placement surfaces, per-feature canary and spatial holdout were not independently replayed. A past run card cannot approve itself.

03 · blocking

Score provenance

The nine scores are user/owner reports without receipts. Past claims of organizer confirmation, hidden-truth inference and control-exempt promotion are superseded.

04 · blocking

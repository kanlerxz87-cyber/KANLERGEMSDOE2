## Qualitative gain rationale (clarified after the stop)

The rank above expresses **design-time preference**, not a measured or forecast score. None has a defensible numerical gain, and every idea could reduce DTI. The rationale was clarified after the geometry stop without changing the frozen lane or budget:

1. **K2-SCALE — moderate potential, moderate implementation cost:** multisensor, multiscale agreement might suppress isolated noise while retaining continuations. If contacts dominate, the added false positives could instead hurt. No holdout was run.
2. **Drainage offsets — potentially moderate but local, high cost:** corroborating a scarp with channel displacement might improve precision in exposed terrain; it may miss buried faults and requires large, verified 1 m coverage.
3. **Relict thermal / temperature — potentially targeted but sparse, high cost:** relict indicators could add evidence in covered settings, but sampling bias and limited product coverage could outweigh any benefit; exact data bytes are not restored.
4. **Magnetic–gravity junctions — uncertain potential, moderate cost:** junction evidence could add unmapped branches, but contact mimics and substantial overlap with past work make both incremental benefit and protocol novelty uncertain.

## Frozen lane and stop policy

Only **K2-SCALE** is selected for this run; the other ideas are not experiments and will not be substituted after a failed guard. Use the existing GEMSDOE33 template's feature builder, `evaluate_holdout.py`, `submission_writer.py`, and shared segment evaluator, pinned to commit `1d99d73fe14d9a87126ffca2db52c69336d6099b`. No private evaluator or writer fork. A proposed nominal full-grid emission budget of **37,654 distinct dots** is fixed before the registry test to match the documented family benchmark; this is a design parameter, not a score.

Before placement: check registry geometry and surface rank correlation; if either makes the literal `>0.90` correlation / `>70%` within 3 pixels rule impossible, record a duplicate and **stop**. For any later run, every actual feature must pass the single-feature AUC canary (two polarities; above 0.90 is suspect), then use whole connected segments, a spatial buffer, visible-only catalogue features and pixel-exact visible masking, pooled DTI and a paired spatial-bootstrap CI. A candidate must beat a same-evaluator current holdout best before a separate selector considers an available weekly slot. A holdout proxy is not a leaderboard prediction. Maximum budget: three experiments or two hours; no competition upload.

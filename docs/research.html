Current prior-art screen · no experiments authorized

Five physically distinct geological mechanisms; zero cleared as novel and eligible

These mechanisms differ physically, but source, script, preregistration, experiment-ledger, and available raster-registry checks found local or peer prior art for all five. They are not labeled “untried,” and none has a numeric HOLDOUT-DTI or leaderboard forecast.

01 · cross-strike structural / hydrothermal · medium–high cost

Ordered basement step, alteration edge, and conductivity boundary

Layers: depth_to_base_surf, gravity/magnetic gradients, GeoDAWN K/Th, cond_surf. A concealed fault may order deep block offset, shallow alteration, and an asymmetric clay-cap edge under alluvium. Mimics: lithologic contacts, playa clays, and survey seams. Prior art: HTCADS/DARP, GEMSDOE24 XFRONT/MTR1, and GEMSDOE52 basement/conductivity families. Official GeoDAWN/GDR links exist, but current official bytes/footprint were not verified; local data cache is absent. Disposition: overlap; not cleared.

02 · potential-field signal structure · low–medium cost

Low-amplitude multi-scale phase ridges

Layers: rtp, tmi_hg/tmi_vg, gravity derivatives, basement depth. Phase-coherent weak edges could retain blind-fault geometry where amplitudes change. Mimics: dikes, lithologic contacts, remanence, seams. Prior art: current GEMSDOE33 PHCONG code and 2026-10-28 runs, plus GEMSDOE45 phase-parity and GEMSDOE26 XEDGE. Computation is modest, but the required competition-band cache is absent. Disposition: implemented/reported elsewhere; not new.

03 · transtensional kinematics · low cost

Fault-relative extensional dilation share

Layers: geod_dilaterate, geod_shearrate, geod_2ndinv, visible-only structural orientation. Relay-zone extension may differ from shear along concealed faults. Mimics: magmatic inflation, groundwater loading, and smooth GNSS artifacts. Prior art: local HTCADS, DARP, and FVCA hypothesis/experiment records, with related peer work. Bands are not cached locally and their official bytes were not refreshed. Disposition: existing local mechanism family; not untried.

04 · geomorphic expression · medium–high cost

Alluvial-fan channel vorticity and knickzones

Layers: det_elev, slope, USGS 3DEP DEM / LiDAR. Multiple channel deflections may preserve low-slip fault traces after scarps erode. Mimics: capture, lithology, shorelines, roads, and ditches. Prior art: exact local HTCADS H-K4 and ECPU H-N3 preregistrations; local GMV D8 features and peer DKRn1/piedmont work. H-K4 is registered/deferred; no completed H-K4 run was located. USGS 3DEP source is public, but no current tile was verified. Disposition: registered, not cleared.

05 · temporal hydrogeodesy · high cost / unavailable here

Seasonal OPERA deformation across an aquifer barrier

Layers: NASA OPERA DISP-S1 time series, cond_surf, gravity-gradient structure. A fault-gouge barrier may create a repeatable seasonal displacement phase step on flat basin floors. Mimics: pumping, compaction, atmosphere, decorrelation, and LOS geometry. Prior art: already deferred as local HTCADS H-K5 / OPERA-poroelastic preregistration; no usable tile, time series, or local run was found. NASA OPERA is an official starting point, but host access and footprint data are not verified. Disposition: deferred/outside available data lane.

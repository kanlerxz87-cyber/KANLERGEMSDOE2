Prioritized, untested hypotheses

Physical signals, not score forecasts.

These are future research directions, not an authorization to leave the assigned lane or fit before the current stop is resolved.

None is validated.

Expected DTI effects are hypotheses, not measured scores. Official access, exact local coverage, scientific usefulness, fold-safe construction and fleet novelty remain open; the detailed preregistered-style slate contains transforms, mimics, costs and access checks.

01 · Highest priority

Height-persistent gravity–magnetic edges

Combine multiscale upward-continued gravity and magnetics with independent hydrothermal evidence; test whether persistent aligned boundaries identify buried structures beyond simple terrain lineaments. Mimic: basin contacts, flight-line artifacts and lithologic edges.

02 · Follow-on

Thermal-transport direction uncertainty

Test whether a family of plausible probe and spring/well transport directions intersects structural ridges. Mimic: groundwater flow, seasonal or land-use temperature anomalies and saline playas.

03 · Follow-on

Visible fault-tip orientation consistency

Compare fold-visible fault tips with potential-field and detrended-elevation gradient orientations. Recompute catalogue features for every split. Mimic: a drainage-aligned escarpment or a continuing lithologic boundary.

04 · Higher access risk

Interferometric strain discontinuity

Test persistent, atmosphere-corrected InSAR displacement gradients with independent geometry. Mimic: aquifer pumping, subsidence and atmospheric delay; exact OPERA tiles and access have not been verified.

Primary sources

Why these signals merit investigation

  • USGS Nevada play-fairway study ↗ describes a blind geothermal case with a structural transfer zone, gravity gradients, magnetic low, temperature and resistivity anomalies. It motivates corroboration, not a DTI gain.
  • USGS GeoDAWN ↗ lists airborne magnetic and radiometric data (CC0); survey artifacts and local coverage must be tested.
  • DOE GDR INGENIOUS compilation ↗ includes fault, thermal, well and related datasets (CC BY 4.0); licensing of derived layers and leakage still require checks.
  • USGS 3DEP ↗ describes free, unrestricted elevation products; exact tiles and preprocessing are not yet verified.
  • NASA OPERA DISP-S1 ↗ describes North American displacement products openly shared without restriction; exact competition-area tiles, access and fitness for these hypotheses are not established.

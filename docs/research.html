What the raster bytes show

What remains unknown

Thinning might remove unhelpful dots, but it can also remove dots that match hidden faults. Staff say the known-fault mask is pixel-exact; nearby unmasked predictions are scored normally, not automatically false positives. The hidden labels, file-specific organizer receipt, and original replay are unavailable. We cannot establish that a new file will exceed 0.2778.

Ranked before modeling

Four hypotheses to investigate

Qualitative ranking only; not fitted here. Novelty across the entire fleet is unproven.

01 · medium cost

Height-persistent cross-gradients

Compare magnetic and gravity ridge orientation and displacement across continuation heights, with hydrothermal corroboration. Could reveal buried steps without scarps. Mimics: unfaulted contacts and survey seams.

HTCADS already uses cross-gradients; multiscale stability requires an explicit ablation.

02 · medium cost

Thermal direction uncertainty

GDR 2 m probes and well/spring chemistry plus terrain and structural orientation; test a fixed family of transport directions. Mimics: groundwater flow, land-use anomalies and saline playas.

03 · low–medium cost

Visible-tip orientation agreement

Recompute visible-only tip geometry per fold and compare with potential-field and terrain lineaments. Mimics: erosion and lithologic boundaries.

04 · high cost · access unverified

Deformation–structure discordance

Test NASA OPERA DISP-S1 displacement gradients against GeoDAWN and 3DEP boundaries. Mimics: pumping and atmospheric/line-of-sight artifacts. Relevant tile availability and access remain unverified.

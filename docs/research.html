THE LEADING HISTORICAL CLAIM

Why might the GEMSDOE32 file have done well?

01

The inspected file is a sparse, catalogue-flank-pruned descendant of a historical dotted family. A 300 m triangular kernel can reward coverage without requiring every prediction to occupy every neighboring pixel. Reducing redundant false-positive mass is therefore a plausible hypothesis, not a measured causal explanation.

The reported 0.2778 is an unverified user/owner report, not an organizer receipt. The private truth is hidden, and the prior reciprocal fit cannot infer true-positive mass or fault prevalence from a few dot counts and rounded values. New fault pixels can also occur near known traces: DrivenData staff clarify the pixel-exact mask ↗. A wider catalogue exclusion can discard real corrections.

01 / MEDIUM COST

Uncertainty-aware thermal orientation

Test probe-gradient direction and uncertainty against gravity/magnetic edges over plausible up-gradient offsets. Mimic: alluvial flow, irrigation or road access.

02 / LOW–MEDIUM COST

Paired-flank magnetic trough

Look for opposite-sign short-wave magnetic slopes corroborated by a gravity edge and survey-resolution sensitivity. Mimic: volcanic contacts and leveling artifacts.

03 / MEDIUM COST

Persistent opposing-scarp asymmetry

Use multi-neighborhood LiDAR face asymmetry and structural direction, with shoreline and road controls. Mimic: terrace risers and berms.

04 / LOW–MEDIUM COST

Alteration contrast across a ridge

Compare K/Th on opposite sides of a structural edge and assess deposit-free ablation. Mimic: lithologic contacts and weathering.

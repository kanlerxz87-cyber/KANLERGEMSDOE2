Forensic analysis

Why the reported 0.2778?

Measured geometry

GEMSDOE32 equals GEMSDOE25's positive mask after removing 6,436 pixels within 2 px of the restored known-fault catalogue. GEMSDOE25 and 30 have identical positive masks; GEMSDOE36 contains all GEMSDOE32 dots plus 1,200.

A plausible, unproven explanation

Pruning may reduce false positives while retaining useful coverage under DTI, but the hidden expert truth and submission receipts are unavailable. Neither causal contribution nor a score ceiling is established. A higher score is possible in principle, but not demonstrated for a new candidate.

Ranked hypotheses

Converging lines of evidence

01 · medium cost · owner mirrors available

Height-persistent cross-gradients

Use magnetic RTP/TMI and isostatic-gravity gradients at multiple continuation heights, plus independent hydrothermal support. Persistent aligned offsets may reveal a buried fault step where a scarp is absent. Mimics: unfaulted contacts and survey seams.

HTCADS already combines cross-gradients and updip projection; multi-height stability needs an explicit ablation and fleet novelty review.

02 · medium cost · GDR mirror available

Thermal direction uncertainty

Test a fixed family of plausible transport directions using 2 m thermal probes, spring/well chemistry, conductivity, terrain and structural ridges. Offset anomalies could indicate a blind feeder. Mimics: shallow groundwater, seasonal heating and saline playas.

Prior HTCADS uses an upslope shift; direction sensitivity is untested. DOE source is public, but mirrored bytes are not authenticated to the official download.

03 · low–medium cost · owner mirrors available

Visible-tip orientation agreement

Recompute fold-visible tip direction and intersect it with potential-field and terrain lineaments. A consistent orientation could trace an unmapped continuation. Mimics: drainage-aligned erosion and lithologic contacts.

Tip cones/relay priors already exist; the proposed cross-layer orientation comparison needs an implementation audit.

04 · high cost · tiles not verified

Deformation–structure discordance

Compare NASA OPERA DISP-S1 displacement gradients across time with GeoDAWN and 3DEP boundaries. Repeated discontinuities might indicate concealed structure. Mimics: pumping subsidence, atmospheric effects and radar line-of-sight artifacts.

NASA describes open U.S. coverage, but relevant tiles, access, dates and local usability remain unverified. Not executable here.

## 1. PhD-Level Forensic Analysis: Why Did `GEMSDOE32` (`e5eb6e7e-zeros`, `0.2778` USER-REPORTED) Achieve the Highest Score Among the 9 Focus Sites, and How Can We Beat It?

### 1.1 Exact Metric Governing Equation (`alpha = 0.2`, `beta = 0.8`, `R = 300 m`)
Per the [official DrivenData GEMS Problem Description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#performance-metric), [NREL/DOE Rules (`96647.pdf`)](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf), and [Organizer Forum Thread 11516](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4), predictions $p(x) \in [0, 1]$ on the `3730 × 3292` `100 m` `EPSG:32611` grid are masked pixel-exactly on known USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue faults (`existing_faults.tif`, `60,988` positive pixels inside the `5,167,373`-pixel active GeoDAWN footprint) and evaluated against hidden expert-labeled fault traces $G$ (`|G| = K`) via the Distance-Weighted Tversky Index:

$$k(d) = \max\!\left(1 - \frac{d}{3\text{ px}},\, 0\right), \qquad \text{TP}_w = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{\|x - g\|_2 \le 3} p(x)\,k(\|x - g\|_2)$$

$$\text{FP}_w = \sum_{x} p(x)\,\Big[1 - \max_{g \in G} k(\|x - g\|_2)\Big], \qquad \text{FN}_w = K - \text{TP}_w$$

$$\text{DTI}(p, G) = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w} = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{0.2\,(\text{TP}_w + \text{FP}_w) + 0.8\,K}$$

### 1.2 Four Structural Reasons `GEMSDOE32` Reached `0.2778` (Verified Byte-for-Byte)
Reopening all 9 hash-pinned focus-site GeoTIFFs (`GEMSDOE24, 25, 27, 28, 30, 31, 32, 33, 36`) establishes four exact geometric facts (with reported scores labeled `USER-REPORTED` because no submission-page receipt image is archived in-repo):

1. **Kernel-Matched Sparse Dotting (`d >= 2.8 px` vs Continuous Ridges, `+0.0678` reported gain)**:
   In the `dti_exact` numerator, $\text{TP}_w$ takes the **pointwise maximum** $\max_{\|x-g\|_2 \le 3} p(x) k(\|x-g\|_2)$ over the 29-pixel `300 m` disk, whereas $\text{FP}_w$ **sums** $p(x)(1 - k(d(x, G)))$ across every emitted pixel! Consequently, a continuous 1-pixel-wide ridge (`19GEMSDOE` `h19-5`, `0.1922`) pays `3x–5x` false-positive mass per unit of recall credit. Spacing binary `1.0` dots at `d = 1.5 px` (`GEMSDOE24`, `60,069` dots -> `0.2477`) and `d = 2.8 px` (`GEMSDOE25` / `GEMSDOE30`, `44,090` identical in-footprint dots -> `0.2600`) eliminates self-overlapping kernel waste while preserving `>= 84%` axial coverage along any fault trace.
2. **Known-Catalogue Collar Pruning (`d_catalogue > 2.0 px`, `+0.0178` reported gain from `0.2600` to `0.2778`)**:
   Exact pixel comparison proves `GEMSDOE32` (`37,654` dots, `0.2778`) is **identically** `GEMSDOE25` (`44,090` dots, `0.2600`) with the `6,436` dots at Euclidean distance $d \le 2.0\text{ px}$ (`200 m`) from `existing_faults.tif` zeroed out (`XOR = 0` pixels across all `5,167,373` footprint cells!). 3. **Strict All-Finite `[0, 1]` Encoding (`-zeros.tif`)**:
   `GEMSDOE32` stores `float32` `0.0` outside the active footprint (`nodata = None`, `0` NaNs across all `12,279,160` pixels, min `0.0`, max `1.0`), avoiding whole-array range/NaN validator failures (`"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"`).
4. **Why `GEMSDOE32` Plateaus at `0.2778` (vs Public `#1 = 0.4544` / `#5 = 0.3566`)**:
   Every raster in the `GEMSDOE24..36` `0.2449–0.2778` cluster is a post-processing variant of the **same underlying `H19-5` ridge map** (all 9 focus leaders share `97.58%–100.00%` directional 3-px overlap with a sibling!).

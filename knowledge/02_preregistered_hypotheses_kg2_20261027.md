2. **Asymmetric Cross-Strike MT Clay-Cap Baffle & Hydrothermal Upflow (`H-N1b`):** Rather than isotropic conductivity gradients that trace playa rims, we compute the signed cross-strike log-conductivity step $\Delta \log\sigma_{\perp} = \overline{\log(1+\sigma)}_{\text{HW}(+3,+6,+9\text{ px})} - \overline{\log(1+\sigma)}_{\text{FW}(-3,-6,-9\text{ px})}$ aligned with basement dip, suppressing flat interior playa brines, and corroborate with up-gradient hydraulic back-projections (`+\nabla \text{det\_elev}`) of GDR `#1391` 2 m temperature probes, geothermometry wells/springs, siliceous sinter vents, K-on-Th metasomatism, magnetite destruction, and USGS/ScienceBase conductive heat-flow residuals (`usgs_heat_flow_wells_epsg32611.csv` + `sb_heat_flow_in_footprint.csv`, fixing `IR-HTCADS-HFZERO-06`).

# Ranked untested hypothesis queue — 2026-10-27

**Status: research proposals only. None was implemented or tested in this review.** The selected HTCADS lane has exhausted its preregistered three-experiment allowance. These proposals are not additions to the frozen preregistration, do not select a live slot, and carry no numeric score projection. Reopen only under a new, approved budget and preregistration.

The ranked list is limited to genuinely new transforms, not H-K1 through H-K5 already present in `registry/preregistration_htcads.json`. H-K1 used the maximum available geothermometer value; H-K2 used upward-continuation crest migration; H-K3 already included static conductivity/seismic-density terms; H-K4 already proposed drainage/knickzone descriptors; H-K5 already proposed InSAR deformation. The proposals below differ as specified.

## Rank 1 — GDR geothermometer discordance / disequilibrium, gated by visible structural evidence

- **Layers / data available:** `.cache/gems_data/external/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv`, columns `temp_c`, `geothermquartz_c`, `geothermchalc_c`, and `geothermcat_c`, plus coordinates and non-label metadata. The restored mirror contains 27,092 rows and 15,168 unique coordinate pairs; non-null counts are 8,718 for measured temperature, 973 for quartz, 966 for chalcedony, and 1,185 for Na-K-Ca. Rows are not necessarily independent wells. The `dist_known_fault_px` column is label-derived and is prohibited as a feature.
- **Target signature / transform:** At each co-located well/spring, compute robust pairwise disagreement among geothermometer estimates (e.g., median absolute deviation and quartz–chalcedony / Na-K-Ca residual contrasts); optionally compute estimate-minus-measured-temperature only where the source definitions make the measurements comparable. Interpolate a sparse confidence-weighted discordance field, then gate/analyze it with the already-defined upgradient/structural evidence. The underlying idea is mixing, boiling, conductive cooling, or water-rock disequilibrium perturbing thermometer agreement near a concealed fluid pathway.
- **Why it may find unmapped faults:** A conduit can produce spatially coherent geochemical disequilibrium or mixing signatures even where topographic scarps and the maximum-geothermometer field are weak. A disagreement surface is not the same signal as H-K1's maximum estimated reservoir temperature.
- **Distinction from repository work:** `src/gems32/htcads.py` currently seeds GDR strength using the maximum of the three geothermometers; it does not model disagreement or its uncertainty. This proposal is a new transform of an existing local table, not a new experiment already in H-K1.
- **Named non-fault mimic:** Non-equilibrium caused by shallow aquifer mixing, boiling/cooling after emergence, sampling/measurement mismatch, or contrasting host lithology; screen using official source metadata where present and require spatially blocked, visible-fault-only gating.
- **Expected improvement vs. cost:** **Potential: moderate, but low-confidence and likely coverage-limited. Cost: low-to-moderate engineering/data cost** because a local mirror exists; official-file/data-dictionary reconciliation and leakage-safe spatial interpolation are still required. No numeric DTI effect is projected.
- **Free official source / availability:** DOE GDR #1391, [INGENIOUS Great Basin compilation](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391), [DOI 10.15121/1881483](https://doi.org/10.15121/1881483). The official record is public and lists CC BY 4.0. The local bytes came from an owner mirror, not directly from GDR in this review; verify source files, field definitions, and reuse terms before running.

## Rank 2 — Landsat SWIR hydrothermal alteration anomaly, normalized for substrate and surface conditions

- **Layers / data availability:** USGS Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 surface reflectance, especially SWIR bands, plus the existing geophysical/geologic predictors for residualization. No Landsat scenes are cached in the repository. Restrict to quality-screened, cloud/shadow/snow-free scenes and seasonally comparable composites.
- **Target signature / transform:** At 30 m, create robust clay/hydroxyl and carbonate/silica-sensitive spectral ratios/indices from atmospherically corrected reflectance; mask vegetation, clouds, shadow, snow, mines/tailings, and playa moisture; remove expected spectral response by broad lithologic class, illumination, slope/aspect, and season; aggregate robust anomaly statistics to the 100 m competition grid. Test the anomaly's spatial continuity and alignment with independent gravity/magnetic/MT structure rather than using a simple raw threshold.
- **Why it may find unmapped faults:** Fault-fed hydrothermal circulation can create alteration halos or mineral assemblages that persist outside obvious fault scarps and are not measured by airborne gamma-ray ratios alone.
- **Distinction from repository work:** Current HTCADS uses airborne radiometric/magnetic grids, MT conductivity, thermal wells/springs, and LiDAR; it has no Landsat SWIR surface-reflectance indices. This is not the already-preregistered InSAR proposal H-K5.
- **Named non-fault mimic:** Exposed lithologic contacts, evaporite playas, mine waste, soil moisture, vegetation, and topographic illumination. Require lithology- and season-aware negative controls; single-index AUC above 0.90 is a leakage/mimic warning until disproven.
- **Expected improvement vs. cost:** **Potential: moderate; cost: medium-to-high** (scene selection, cloud/shadow masks, compositing, atmospheric/terrain effects, 30-to-100 m aggregation). Utility may be poor where alteration is buried or vegetation/soil cover is substantial. No numeric DTI effect is projected.
- **Free official source / availability:** [USGS Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 Surface Reflectance](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-reflectance), [Collection 2 overview](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2), and [EarthExplorer access](https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/). Official product pages and access pathway were checked by source review; no imagery was downloaded here.

## Rank 3 — Time-resolved earthquake migration / swarm geometry from ComCat

- **Layers / data availability:** USGS ANSS Comprehensive Earthquake Catalog (ComCat) event time, hypocenter, magnitude, depth, network, and location-quality fields; optionally use focal mechanisms only where coverage/quality supports it. No ComCat event table is cached in this repository. H-K3 already included static earthquake-density channels; this proposal specifically uses time-order and migration, not another static density raster.
- **Target signature / transform:** For time windows chosen before examining holdout labels, estimate uncertainty-weighted, time-resolved point-process intensity, cross-strike centroid migration, depth progression, and lineament coherence. Compare against stationary/seasonal background and aftershock/cluster nulls; retain only features supported by events whose location uncertainty is meaningfully below the target scale.
- **Why it may find unmapped faults:** A migrating swarm or persistent hypocenter plane can reveal active, buried fault segments and tips that have no surface scarp. Time-resolved motion is information not represented by an aggregate seismicity-density channel.
- **Distinction from repository work:** H-K3 used static seismic density terms; no event-time migration, focal-plane, or depth-time tensor is recorded in the current feature pipeline. The target is active-fault kinematics, not static conductivity.
- **Named non-fault mimic:** Geothermal injection/induced seismicity, volcanic swarms, aftershock sequences, mining/quarry blasts, network changes, and catalog-completeness shifts. Separate or downweight known induced/volcanic sequences; model event uncertainty and catalog regime explicitly.
- **Expected improvement vs. cost:** **Potential: moderate-to-high only in adequately monitored active corridors; cost: high** (official API extraction, completeness/uncertainty review, event de-clustering, temporal feature engineering, and sparse-coverage controls). Many blind or locked faults may be aseismic, so expected value is uncertain. No numeric DTI effect is projected.
- **Free official source / availability:** [USGS ComCat documentation](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/data/comcat/index.php) and [FDSN event API](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/). The official API documentation was inspected; the event service host is outside the sandbox's outbound allowlist, and no query was run.

## Rank 4 — Groundwater-head discontinuity and seasonal phase lag across candidate structures

- **Layers / data availability:** USGS monitoring-location metadata and groundwater-level/field-measurement time series from the modern [USGS Water Data APIs](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/). No monitoring-well time series are cached in this repository; local spatial coverage must be established before feature design.
- **Target signature / transform:** For vetted monitoring wells on opposite sides of an independently defined candidate structure, estimate persistent head-offset, recharge-response lag, and seasonal amplitude/phase discontinuity after controlling for elevation, aquifer class, pumping, well construction, precipitation/recharge, and measurement cadence. Use only a predeclared neighborhood graph and quality flags; do not derive candidates from the hidden/withheld fault labels.
- **Why it may find unmapped faults:** A low-permeability fault core or juxtaposed hydrostratigraphic units can compartmentalize groundwater, creating a head or response discontinuity without a surface scarp.
- **Distinction from repository work:** Existing GDR data are mostly point thermal/geothermometer measurements; this uses time-series groundwater hydraulics and independent USGS monitoring wells. It is not the H-K1 upgradient thermal-probe transform or H-K5 InSAR proposal.
- **Named non-fault mimic:** Aquifer lithology/contact, pumping, irrigation, recharge heterogeneity, well-screen depth, and construction differences. Require matched-well controls, aquifer metadata, and a predeclared minimum support threshold.
- **Expected improvement vs. cost:** **Potential: low-to-moderate and highly coverage-dependent; cost: high** (well discovery, time-series harmonization, pumping/recharge covariates, and spatial support checks). Do not proceed unless the AOI yields enough independent, comparable wells.
- **Free official source / availability:** [USGS Water Data API hub](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/), [API documentation](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/docs/), and [Water Data for the Nation](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/). The official API hub was inspected; no AOI query was made and the modern API host is outside the sandbox's outbound allowlist.

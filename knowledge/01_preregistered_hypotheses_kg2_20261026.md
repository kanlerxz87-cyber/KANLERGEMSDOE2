## 3. Preregistered 3-Experiment Plan & Mandatory Decision Gates

To respect the hard cap (`<= 3` experiments, `<= 2` hours) with zero post-hoc cherry-picking:

1. **Pre-Experiment Gate 0 — Two-Polarity Single-Feature Leakage Canary:**
   - Every candidate input feature in `KG2-MGHV` is evaluated alone on all 4 `SegmentHoldout` folds (`two_polarity_auc = max(AUC, 1 - AUC)`).
   - **Hard Gate:** `max two-polarity AUC <= 0.90`. Any feature `> 0.90` halts immediately as leakage.

2. **Experiment 1 (`EXP-1`: `KG2-Core-Gap10m-Junction`) — Multi-Scale 10 m / 3 m / 1 m Gap-Bridged Piedmont-Junction + All-19-Band Out-of-Fold Classifier:**
   - Tests adding the label-free 10 m USGS 3DEP scarp stack (`100%` coverage across the `1.27M`-px LiDAR gap), 3 m kinematic scarp facing (`KIN_3m`), concave-up piedmont-junction lines (`J_100m`, `J_10m`), and the 6 omitted competition bands to the strictly out-of-fold 2-regime detector (`37,654` budget).
   - Evaluates against same-evaluator controls: `B1_oof_base_submod`, `B0_oof_h24_ridge`, `JUNC_100m_only` (the label-free piedmont-junction physics baseline), and `DPRF1_recorded` (`0.163792`).

3. **Experiment 2 (`EXP-2`: `KG2-DualHead-HydroVent`) — Adding Contrarian Hydrothermal Magnetite-Destruction, Geothermal Vent Upflow, Gallardo–Meju Cross-Gradient & Dual-Head 300 m Soft-DTI Kernel Expectancy:**
   - Tests whether blending the strictly out-of-fold **300 m Triangular-Kernel Soft-DTI Regressor** (`HistGradientBoostingRegressor` trained only on visible-fault 300 m kernel support per fold) with the Elkan-calibrated Core Classifier AND adding the shallow hydrothermal demagnetization / vent / cross-gradient feature block improves pooled `HOLDOUT-DTI` over `EXP-1`.

4. **Experiment 3 (`EXP-3`: `KG2-ThreeRegime-CELF`) — Three-Regime (Exposed/Gap Scarp Junctions + Low-Slope `<= 6°` Piedmont Splays + Concealed Geothermal/Basin Conduits) Submodular 300 m CELF Allocation & Matched-Random Control:**
   - Tests the preregistered **Three-Regime Submodular 300 m CELF Allocator** (`54%` Exposed & 10m-Gap-Bridged Range-Front Junctions, `22%` Low-Slope `<= 6°` Alluvial Piedmont & Transtensional Splays, `24%` Concealed Basin `depth > 150 m` & Geothermal Vent Upflow Conduits, `min_dist_px = 3.0`, total budget `37,654` dots) plus a matched-count uniform off-catalogue random-dot null control (`CTRL_RAND_37654`) on `SegmentHoldout`.
   - Verifies both **holdout DTI** and **novelty gates** (`|Spearman rho| <= 0.90` on continuous surface BEFORE placement AND on final emitted dots; `<= 70%` directional 3-px dot overlap against all `359` competing detector rasters across all 9 focus sites + registry, including `DPRF1`, `H31-X`, `H31-1`, and `B2`).

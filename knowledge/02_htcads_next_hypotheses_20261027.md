## Data and verification boundaries

- The official [USGS GeoDAWN release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) describes the magnetic/radiometric grids and coordinated lidar; it is marked CC0. The owner-derived uint8 caches are not themselves the official release and quantisation may affect sign/contrast interpretations.
- The [DOE Geothermal Data Repository INGENIOUS compilation](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) describes thermal probes, well/spring chemistry, paleogeothermal features and other layers; its record is CC BY 4.0. The relevant cached owner mirrors and hashes are listed in `registry/data_manifest.json` and were restored locally. Their inputs and transforms still require independent provenance checks.
- The official [problem description and metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) establishes the fault-discovery task, but does not establish that any of the above anomalies is a fault.
- No new external source is needed for these conditional ideas. The public source pages were reachable through the review tool; direct downloads from their hosts are not available to this sandbox. The existing GitHub mirrors were restored and hash checked. Rights and suitability of each derivative remain to be audited.
- Rank 1 is the conditional top candidate. Do not fit or emit it until it can pass the literal all-entry pre-placement registry check. If this check proves impossible, stop without canaries, fit, or a new GeoTIFF; do not change dots or remove a registry entry to evade the gate. If it later becomes possible, run every input as a one-feature leakage canary first and compare the candidate with a freshly replayed same-evaluator current best using the shared whole-component, buffered, pooled DTI evaluator, a paired spatial-block CI, and the shared submission writer. Maximum three experiments / two hours; no portal slot in this lane.

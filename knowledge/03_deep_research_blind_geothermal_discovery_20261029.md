## Confirmed discovery precedents

1. **Southeastern Gabbs Valley, western Nevada — blind high-temperature system.**
   Play fairway analysis over an area with no previous geothermal exploration
   found collocated intersecting gravity gradients, a magnetic low, low
   resistivity, a 2 m temperature anomaly, and 130–140 °C geothermometers from
   nearby agricultural wells. Six 150 m gradient holes were drilled; two reached
   114.5 °C and 124.9 °C bottom-hole temperatures, establishing the discovery.
   Estimated P50 capacity ~16 MWe.
   [ScienceDirect — Gabbs Valley blind geothermal system](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0375650521001371)

2. **"Big Blind", western Nevada — first blind commercial geothermal discovery in the US in 30+ years (announced Dec 2025).**
   No surface geothermal manifestations and no prior exploration/well data.
   Zanskar paired geologists with an AI prediction engine, then drilled two
   intermediate-depth wells (July–August 2025); the second reached ~2668.5 ft TD
   with max equilibration temperature ~250 °F, lost circulation, and fractured
   core showing open fractures — massive reservoir permeability. Dozens of
   similar AI-identified anomalies exist; fieldwork confirmation is the gate.
   [Zanskar Substack](https://zanskargeothermal.substack.com/p/zanskar-proves-first-blind-geothermal) ·
   [Renewable Energy Magazine](https://www.renewableenergymagazine.com/geothermal/zanskar-reveals-discovery-of-first-blind-geothermal-20251204) ·
   [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/12/04/1128763/ai-geothermal-zanskar/)

3. **Mountain Home Air Force Base, Idaho — blind high-temperature system without mapped faults or surface manifestations.**
   Initial investigations prompted by elevated regional heat flow; slim-hole
   drilling program used to measure temperature/permeability distribution,
   system boundaries, upflow/outflow/recharge — an intermediate option between
   temperature-gradient holes and full reservoir testing.
   [Stanford PANGEA proceedings, 45th Workshop on Geothermal Reservoir Engineering](https://pangea.stanford.edu/ERE/db/GeoConf/papers/SGW/2020/Nielson.pdf)

## Discovery workflow relevant to this submission

- **Play fairway analysis**: integrate and weight multiple datasets (geology,
  gravity, magnetics, magnetotelluric/resistivity, 2 m temperature, fluid
  geochemistry) to rank prospects on heat, permeability, and fluid.
- **Structural controls**: blind systems sit on concealed faults/fracture zones
  with no surface scarp; slip/dilation tendency and cross-section analysis help
  target the conduit (matches this session's "geothermal blind conduit" target).
- **Confirmation requires drilling**: geophysics + anomalies rank targets; only
  downhole temperature/permeability data prove a system. Analog: this session's
  candidate is a research-only ranking surface — no holdout replay can substitute
  for organizer-scored truth, hence the negative verdict.
- **AI prospecting + field confirmation**: models trained on known systems and
  simulations predict hot spots; fieldwork (wells) confirms. This session reused
  the cached feature stack and a holdout screen only — consistent with
  "research only, not cleared to submit."

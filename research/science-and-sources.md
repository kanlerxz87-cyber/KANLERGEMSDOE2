## Why the historical H33 emission is plausible, not proven optimal

The user-reported GEMSDOE32 result is the largest of the nine supplied historical results, but no submission-page receipt tying the exact raster hash to that value has been located. The peer's build record describes a sparse D2.8 lineage with a B=2 known-catalogue flank removal. Under the official metric, each ground-truth pixel receives its best nearby prediction's triangular-kernel credit; an extra prediction can add false-positive mass without improving already-covered truth. Sparse placement can therefore reduce redundancy while preserving some coverage. Removing predictions near mapped traces can alter the precision/coverage tradeoff, but can also remove genuine undiscovered faults. Without hidden ground truth, its causal contribution and whether any new method can exceed the reported value are unknown. Public leaderboard performance is not final private-round performance.

The isolated marginal-credit identity in the shared template is `k > alpha * current_DTI` **only** for one unit prediction adding credit `k` to one previously uncovered truth pixel, with `alpha + beta = 1`. It is not a general rule for overlapping kernels, uncertain truth, or changes in several pixels. The former template helper used a different expression and is corrected in [template-fixes.md](template-fixes.md); the actual shared DTI calculation was not modified.

## Known caveats requiring follow-up

- The mirrored sample is not organizer-authenticated. It contains positive values matching the known labels, while the official page calls its sample a total-fault-absence example. Use the mirror only for footprint/grid, never as a feature or hidden label; compare against a genuinely authenticated organizer sample when available.
- The public competition data page redirects to login. Peer SHA-256 pins establish reproducible equality to the mirror, not official authorship or unrestricted rights to every derivative.
- The previous E1 claims used non-whole-component quadrant splits, a fixed full-catalogue-pruned map for a hide/recover comparison, and an incomplete nine-file registry that omitted the lattice; the new independent witness shows its dots also fail the literal rule against a registered continuous raster. These claims and their numerical projections are not current evidence.
- The user supplied no identifiable exclusive method paragraph. Multiple peer projects claim different shared templates. Do not assert lane exclusivity or silently change the registry definition.
- An organizer/portal result is not confirmed for any new TIFF in this session. No new prediction, holdout score, leaderboard projection or competition upload was made.

### Why candidate 1 ranks first—and why it is still not being run

It directly addresses a physical source of spatial displacement between a geothermal surface manifestation and its deeper feeder. An uncertainty ensemble is scientifically preferable to assuming one exact flow vector, but only if the flow directions/dispersivities are preregistered from physical ranges rather than optimized against withheld labels. The feature must be built fold-safely from visible-only catalogue information, separately canaried on the buffered whole-segment holdout, and compared in a paired way to the same baseline and evaluator. None of those new calculations was run: the mandatory novelty gate is earlier in the prescribed sequence and already fails. Calling this a winner or assigning a score would be unsupported.

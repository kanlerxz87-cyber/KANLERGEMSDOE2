## Interpretation and gate status

1. The highest qualitative priority is R1 because it tests a known physical limitation in the current implementation: a terrain-gradient proxy is being used where actual groundwater head is unavailable. This is not a predicted DTI gain, a new submission score, or a conclusion that the proxy is wrong at every site.
2. Before these variants could be tested, a separate written preregistration would need to specify the exact one-method comparison, segment buffers, feature-alone AUC canaries, negative controls, current same-evaluator references, selection rule, and data hashes. The already-run three-arm HTCADS lane cannot be extended by silently calling the slate an ablation.
3. No HOLDOUT-DTI is available for these candidates. The complete live registry and pre-placement surface have not been replayed. A single lattice witness is sufficient to stop the frozen 37,654-dot experiment; no smaller budget or registry exemption is introduced here.
4. No numerical leaderboard estimate is defensible. The 0.2778 GEMSDOE32 value is USER/OWNER-REPORTED without an organizer receipt linking it to the pinned TIFF. A score projection is not a score.

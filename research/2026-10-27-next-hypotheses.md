Ranking below is qualitative scientific judgment, **not an estimated score**. A different scientific lane would require a new assignment.

The competition asks for **faults**, not proof of geothermal vents. The [official problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) describes incomplete training labels, 100 m features and a separate expert-labeled test set. [DOE's Great Basin inventory](https://www.osti.gov/dataexplorer/biblio/dataset/1148722) supports considering fault tips, intersections and stepovers in geothermal exploration, not treating every thermal anomaly as a fault. [USGS GeoDAWN](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) documents magnetic/radiometric surveys and changing survey resolution/flight height; [DOE GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) lists the thermal probes, geothermal deposits and other layers. The official GDR page advertises the archives as public, with CC BY 4.0 attribution. Official-host bytes were **not** downloaded in this sandbox: the locally restored files are hash-pinned third-party mirrors, so identity with the official files remains unverified.

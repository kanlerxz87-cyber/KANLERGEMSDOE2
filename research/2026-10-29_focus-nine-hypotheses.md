## Screened mechanisms

### 1. Cross-strike basement–alteration–conductivity ordering

- **Layers / proposed measurement:** `depth_to_base_surf`; gravity horizontal/vertical gradients (`iso_grav_anom_hg`, `iso_grav_anom_vg`); magnetic `tmi`, `tmi_vg` and, where provenance allows, GeoDAWN `TMI_up150`; GeoDAWN radiometric K/Th; `cond_surf`; fold-visible fault orientation only. The proposed discriminator is a predeclared, signed order of a basement step, shallow magnetic/alteration edge, and one-sided conductivity boundary—not a generic anomaly sum.
- **Mechanism / blind-fault rationale:** a buried fault or relay may juxtapose basement blocks, focus hydrothermal magnetite destruction/potassic alteration, and produce an asymmetric clay-cap or fluid boundary beneath alluvium. Agreement in ordered, independent layers could survive where no topographic scarp is exposed.
- **Named mimics:** unfaulted lithologic/dike contacts, conductive playa clay or saline groundwater, survey-leveling seams, and alteration unrelated to active permeability. These require lithology/seam controls and independent-layer ablations; coincidence alone is not proof of a fault.
- **Exact difference from prior work / disposition:** the exact three-edge sign/order is narrower than the individual existing transforms, but the physical family is already present in local [HTCADS-K2](../src/gems32/htcads.py) (cross-gradient, magnetic trough, K-on-Th, MT boundary) and [DARP-MT10](../src/gems32/darp_mt10.py) (signed cross-strike MT step), and in the pinned peer [GEMSDOE24 H24-XFRONT preregistration](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE24/blob/5e3fa8ff58acddb2d95a1eed30ca98a40ff14421/knowledge/25_preregistered_XFRONT_2026-10-26.md) / [results](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE24/blob/5e3fa8ff58acddb2d95a1eed30ca98a40ff14421/knowledge/28_XFRONT_findings_and_9site_executive_summary_2026-10-26.md) and [GEMSDOE52 H93-ABS/H93-COND preregistration](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE52/blob/47f575c137d576e5664ceca11bea4008f235fa55/knowledge/81_hypotheses_H93_preregistered.md) / [results](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE52/blob/47f575c137d576e5664ceca11bea4008f235fa55/knowledge/82_h93_results_and_limits.md). Its emitted raster also fails its literal 3-px gate (72.75% >70%). This is an overlapping prior, not a transferable leaderboard result. Do not call this proposed family untried.

The bands are part of the competition stack and the related USGS GeoDAWN / DOE GDR #1391 inputs have official landing pages ([GeoDAWN](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and), [GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391)); neither official bytes nor current footprint coverage were re-verified. ### 2. Low-amplitude, multi-scale potential-field phase ridges

- **Layers / proposed measurement:** competition `rtp`, `tmi_hg`, `tmi_vg`, `iso_grav_anom_hg`, `iso_grav_anom_vg`, and `depth_to_base_surf`; compare phase-coherent ridge location/orientation across scales and independent fields, with visible-fault masks derived inside each training fold.
- **Mechanism / blind-fault rationale:** a buried discontinuity may have a stable edge phase even when its anomaly amplitude is weak or varies across the magnetic and gravity transforms, allowing a subdued fault boundary to be ranked beneath basin cover.
- **Named mimics:** lithologic contacts and dikes, remanent magnetization, cultural lineaments, and survey seams. Phase coherence is not geological identity; independent-field agreement and seam controls are essential.
- **Exact difference from prior work / disposition:** phase congruency is a signal transform, distinct from the geological layers in item 1, but it is **already implemented and experimentally reported** in pinned [GEMSDOE33 PHCONG code](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE33/blob/3eaf85efb49f364b84f8be04e8b084b12d0303b9/src/gemsdoe33/phcong.py) and [results](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE33/blob/3eaf85efb49f364b84f8be04e8b084b12d0303b9/knowledge/30_phcong_results_20261028.md); close analogues are [GEMSDOE45 phase-parity runner](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE45/blob/f90a332ab577877eaa4f533748cc1a24dc58741d/scripts/run_experiment.py) and [GEMSDOE26 XEDGE](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE26/blob/ba071a372c970531fca4d024b7fc06631d9ce604/src/gems26/xedge.py). PHCONG's peer-reported negative is explicit: `gems-segment-pooled-v1`, 60,988 withheld positives, confirmation 0.170588 [0.163996, 0.176581]; paired Δ vs matched-random −0.011098 [−0.016260, −0.006098], 0/4 folds, so promotion was withdrawn. ### 3. Fault-relative extensional dilation share

- **Layers / proposed measurement:** `geod_dilaterate`, `geod_shearrate`, `geod_2ndinv`, plus a fold-visible structural orientation and potential-field ridge context. A possible predeclared statistic would contrast positive dilatation with shear magnitude along a visible-only structural corridor, rather than using raw dilatation alone.
- **Mechanism / blind-fault rationale:** transtensional step-overs and relay zones can partition extension differently from strike-parallel shear; a coherent dilatational corridor aligned with a buried structural edge could mark a concealed fault segment.
- **Named mimics:** magmatic or hydrothermal inflation, groundwater loading, broad GNSS interpolation artifacts, and regional strain gradients. Require a shear-only control and spatial-block tests.
- **Exact difference from prior work / disposition:** a shear-normalized share conditioned on a visible-only structural corridor is not byte-for-byte the existing transform, but the core mechanism is **not new here**: [HTCADS-K2](../src/gems32/htcads.py) combines shear and dilatation; [DARP-MT10](../src/gems32/darp_mt10.py) uses regional slip/dilation tendency; [FVCA hypotheses](fvca_hypotheses.md), its [review](fvca_review.md), the [local FVCA experiment ledger](../evidence/fvca_experiments.json), and [runner](../scripts/run_fvca_experiments.py) already screen and test strain/mechanics variants. Peer [GEMSDOE26 DILCOND-v1](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE26/blob/ba071a372c970531fca4d024b7fc06631d9ce604/src/gems26/dilcond.py) is an adjacent but distinct sign-aware positive-dilatation × positive-conductivity local-anomaly coincidence at 300/600/1200 m (no shear-share ratio); its build/holdout and test files are in that pinned repo. This exact difference does not clear the mechanism family as new. Do not relabel it as untried.
- **Cost / official-source access:** low compute if geodetic bands are cached; no local feature cache is present. The bands belong to the competition input stack, whose current official bytes require authenticated access and were not refreshed here.
- **Expected HOLDOUT-DTI / leaderboard effect:** **not estimated**.

### 4. Alluvial-fan drainage-azimuth vorticity and knickzones

- **Layers / proposed measurement:** `det_elev`, `det_elev_slope`, and, if obtained and provenance-checked, USGS 3DEP 10 m DEM / 1 m LiDAR. Derive a drainage network, cross-strike channel-azimuth rotation, longitudinal-profile knickzones, and paired-bank offsets; do not infer faults from one channel bend.
- **Mechanism / blind-fault rationale:** a low-slip oblique fault beneath an alluvial fan can deflect ephemeral washes or generate aligned knickzones after a tall scarp has been eroded or buried, leaving a geomorphic trace across a piedmont.
- **Named mimics:** stream capture, resistant-bedrock boundaries, playa shorelines, roads/rail embankments, and irrigation ditches. Require matched lithology/road/shoreline controls and several independent channels.
- **Exact difference from prior work / disposition:** this is **already explicitly preregistered locally** as HTCADS `H-K4` and carried into ECPU `H-N3` (“AFDK … drainage-azimuth vorticity / knickzone tensor”) in [`registry/preregistration_htcads.json`](../registry/preregistration_htcads.json) and [`registry/preregistration_ecpu_20261027.json`](../registry/preregistration_ecpu_20261027.json). The H-K4 entry is marked `selected_for_validation: false`; targeted local script/ledger searches found no completed H-K4 experiment, so its status is *registered/deferred*, not “untried.” Local GMV D8/valley features and pinned peer [GEMSDOE24 DKRn1 code](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE24/blob/5e3fa8ff58acddb2d95a1eed30ca98a40ff14421/src/gems/dkrn1.py) / [protocol and findings](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE24/blob/5e3fa8ff58acddb2d95a1eed30ca98a40ff14421/knowledge/27_preregistered_DKRN1_2026-10-26.md), plus GEMSDOE27/31 piedmont/relief work, are close analogues. ### 5. Seasonal InSAR displacement phase across a fault-controlled aquifer barrier

- **Layers / proposed measurement:** a footprint-matched NASA/JPL OPERA DISP-S1 displacement time series, `cond_surf`, and gravity-gradient structure. Compare repeatable cross-fault seasonal-amplitude/phase discontinuity with static structure; do not treat line-of-sight displacement as fault slip.
- **Mechanism / blind-fault rationale:** a fault-gouge or clay smear can impede groundwater flow; seasonal hydraulic loading may produce a spatially sharp deformation/phase lag across a buried fault on a nearly flat basin floor where topography provides little evidence.
- **Named mimics:** pumping/irrigation bowls, aquifer compaction, atmospheric delay, decorrelation, and line-of-sight geometry. Require temporal repeatability, coherence masks, and infrastructure controls.
- **Exact difference from prior work / disposition:** the observable is temporal InSAR rather than static strain, but this is **already present as a deferred local prior hypothesis** in HTCADS-K2 H-K5 ([frozen HTCADS preregistration](../registry/preregistration_htcads.json)) and in the [2026-10-27 ECPU/OPERA preregistration](../registry/preregistration_ecpu_20261027.json). No local OPERA time-series implementation or completed experiment was found in the targeted source/script/ledger search; it remains deferred and outside the currently available data lane, not a cleared untried idea.
- **Cost / official-source access:** high (tile selection, orbit/LOS alignment, coherence and time-series preprocessing). NASA OPERA is the official starting point ([OPERA](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/opera)); required footprint tile/acquisition set was not verified or downloaded, the official host is outside this sandbox’s egress allowlist, and no official provenance is recorded for any candidate raster because none exists.

## Novelty versus the archived local experiment

The archived E1 method used supervised HGB with magnetic–gravity cross-gradient, Hessian/tilt responses, radiometric ratios and LiDAR strengths. None of the four proposals implements that same mechanism: candidate 1 estimates patch translation, 2 models signed along-strike step changes, 3 pools signed drainage displacement, and 4 tests paired alteration-shoulder geometry. These distinctions are algorithmic hypotheses, **not a complete-registry novelty certificate**. Candidate 1 remains the qualitative expected-benefit leader among cached-data options, with medium/high implementation cost; there is no defensible numerical expected DTI gain.

What actually happened

A stop, not a shortcut.

Scientific and submission gates are separate. Passing a file-format check is never the same as demonstrating a likely high score.

The overlap constraint

One registry raster is enough to stop this lane.

Only 6,649 footprint cells lie outside the registered lattice's three-pixel halo. With 37,654 distinct dots, at least 31,005 would necessarily be inside it.
